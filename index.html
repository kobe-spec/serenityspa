About

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need a consultation before my first treatment?

No separate visit is needed. Every appointment starts with a short intake where we go over your goals, check that the treatment is suitable, and answer anything you are unsure about before we begin.

How many sessions will I need to see a change?

Contouring treatments build over a series rather than appearing after one visit. The number depends on the area and your starting point, and we will give you an honest estimate at your first appointment. Results vary from person to person.

Are these treatments medical procedures?

No. Serenity Spa provides non-medical wellness and cosmetic services. Nothing we offer diagnoses or treats a medical condition, and none of it replaces care from your doctor.

Is there any downtime?

No. Every treatment on our menu is non-invasive and you can drive yourself home and return to your day. Light activity after contouring sessions is encouraged.

What should I do before and after my appointment?

Hydrate well the day before and the day of, eat something light, and avoid alcohol for twenty-four hours. Afterward, keep drinking water and add a short walk if you have had contouring or sauna work.

How do I book, and can I pay at the appointment?

Book online through our booking page or call us. Payment is taken at your appointment; package plans are paid up front and used across several visits.
