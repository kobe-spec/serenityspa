About this treatment

What teeth whitening is

Sparkly Whites is a cosmetic whitening session using a professional whitening gel activated by LED light. It targets surface discolouration from coffee, tea, red wine, tobacco and everyday food, and it is a popular add-on before weddings, photos and events for clients around Castle Hayne. Nothing is drilled or filed, and the session takes seventy-five minutes from start to finish. This is a cosmetic service, not dentistry — it does not replace a dental check-up or treat any dental condition.

Benefits

  • Lifts surface staining from coffee, tea, wine and tobacco
  • Brightens in a single seventy-five minute appointment
  • No needles, drilling or dental work involved
  • Gel and LED strength adjusted for sensitive teeth
  • Pairs easily with another service on the same visit

Your appointment

What to expect

  1. Shade checkWe record your starting shade so you can see the difference at the end.
  2. PrepLips and gums are protected and a retractor is fitted so the gel stays on the teeth.
  3. Gel and LED cyclesWhitening gel is applied and the LED light runs in timed cycles, with fresh gel between each one.
  4. Rinse and shade compareEverything is removed, you rinse, and we compare against your starting shade.
  5. AftercareWe go over the first twenty-four hours — what to avoid so the result holds.

Questions

Teeth Whitening FAQ

How white will my teeth get?

It depends entirely on your starting shade and what caused the staining. We record your shade before and after so the change is measurable rather than guessed at. Results vary.

Will it make my teeth sensitive?

Some clients notice short-term sensitivity that settles within a day. Tell us beforehand if your teeth are already sensitive and we will adjust.

What can I eat afterward?

Avoid coffee, tea, red wine, tomato sauce, berries and tobacco for twenty-four hours. Stick to lighter-coloured food while the enamel re-settles.

Does this work on crowns or veneers?

No. Whitening only affects natural tooth enamel. Restorations keep their existing shade, which can leave a mismatch — worth discussing before you book.

Is this a dental treatment?

No. It is a cosmetic whitening service and is not a substitute for care from a licensed dentist.
