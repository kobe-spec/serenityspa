About this treatment

What vacuum butt therapy is

Vacuum butt therapy uses controlled suction cups moved over the glutes and upper thighs. The suction draws blood to the surface, stimulates circulation, and is designed to temporarily lift and plump the area while encouraging collagen activity in the skin. It is non-surgical, involves no injections or fillers, and there is nothing to recover from afterward. Clients in Castle Hayne and Wilmington choose it as an alternative to surgical enhancement. Like most contouring work, it responds best to a series rather than one appointment.

Benefits

  • Designed to temporarily lift and plump the glutes
  • Stimulates blood flow and lymphatic movement in the area
  • May improve the appearance of dimpling and uneven texture
  • Supports collagen activity for firmer-looking skin
  • No injections, implants or fillers involved
  • No recovery time — you leave and carry on with your day

Your appointment

What to expect

  1. ConsultationWe look at the area together, discuss the shape you are after, and confirm nothing rules out suction treatment.
  2. Prep and oilThe area is cleansed and oiled so the cups glide rather than pull on the skin.
  3. Vacuum cupping passesSuction cups move in sweeping and circular patterns across the glutes and upper thighs. Suction strength is adjusted to your comfort throughout.
  4. Static holdCups are held in position on specific points to concentrate the effect.
  5. Finish and aftercareWe wipe down, review aftercare, and book your next session in the series if you want one.

Questions

Vacuum Butt Therapy FAQ

How long does the lift last?

The visible effect is temporary and fades over days. A completed series holds longer than a single session, which is why most clients book the package plan. Results vary.

Will it bruise me?

Light marking can happen, especially on the first visit. We start with lower suction and build up. Tell us immediately if pressure feels sharp rather than firm.

Can I work out the same day?

Yes. Light movement is fine and helps circulation. There is no restriction on activity afterward.

What should I wear?

Bring or wear something you are comfortable being treated in. Disposable garments are available if you prefer.

Can I combine it with cavitation?

Yes — the combined cavitation and vacuum butt therapy package pairs the two in one longer appointment.
