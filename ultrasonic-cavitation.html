About this treatment

What ultrasonic cavitation is

Our signature session combines three treatments in one visit. Ultrasonic cavitation uses low-frequency sound waves to target stubborn fat deposits, body sculpting works the treated area, and lymphatic massage encourages your body’s natural drainage to move waste and excess fluid. Clients across Castle Hayne book this package when diet and exercise have plateaued and they want a non-surgical option with no recovery time. Each appointment runs an hour and thirty-five minutes, and changes are designed to build gradually over a series rather than all at once.

Benefits

  • Targets stubborn fat deposits on the stomach, flanks, thighs and arms
  • Supports skin tightening and may soften the look of cellulite
  • Encourages circulation and lymphatic drainage after the cavitation pass
  • May reduce bloating and water retention in the treated area
  • Supports collagen production through red light exposure
  • Non-surgical and painless, with no downtime afterward

Your appointment

What to expect

  1. Intake and measurementsWe talk through your goals, review anything that would make treatment unsuitable, and take starting measurements of the area.
  2. Ultrasonic cavitationGel is applied and the cavitation handpiece moves over the area in slow passes. Most clients hear a faint high-pitched tone and feel warmth.
  3. Body sculpting passRadio frequency and red light are worked over the same area to support skin firmness.
  4. Lymphatic massageManual drainage massage moves fluid toward the lymph nodes so the area is not left congested.
  5. Hydration and aftercareWe finish with water and a short aftercare plan — hydration, light movement, and when to come back.

Questions

Ultrasonic Cavitation FAQ

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the area and your starting point. Most clients book a series rather than a single visit, which is why the package plan exists. We will give you an honest estimate at your first appointment.

Does ultrasonic cavitation hurt?

No. Most clients describe it as warm and pressured, similar to a firm massage. You may hear a faint ringing tone during the cavitation pass, which is normal and stops when the handpiece lifts.

What should I do before my appointment?

Drink plenty of water the day before and the morning of, eat lightly, and avoid alcohol for 24 hours. Come in loose clothing and skip lotion on the treatment area.

Is there any downtime?

None. You can drive yourself home and return to your day. Light activity such as a walk after your session is encouraged to keep lymph moving.

Will the results last?

Changes are not permanent on their own. Hydration, movement and a steady routine make a real difference in how long your shape holds. Results vary from person to person.
