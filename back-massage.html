About this treatment

What back massage is

A full seventy-five minutes spent entirely on your back — no rushed full-body circuit. The work moves through the shoulders, along either side of the spine, and into the lower back, using kneading, stretching and sustained pressure on the spots that are actually tight. Pressure is set by you and adjusted as we go. Castle Hayne clients book this for desk-work shoulders, driving stiffness and the general accumulation of a physical job. It is therapeutic rather than ceremonial.

Benefits

  • Eases tightness through the shoulders, traps and lower back
  • Sustained time on problem areas instead of a quick pass
  • Pressure calibrated to you and adjusted throughout
  • Supports circulation and general muscular recovery
  • A straightforward reset for stress held in the upper body

Your appointment

What to expect

  1. Where it hurtsWe ask what is tight, what is painful, and what you want left alone.
  2. Warm-upBroad, slower strokes warm the tissue before any deeper work starts.
  3. Targeted workKneading and sustained pressure on the areas you flagged, with stretching through the shoulders.
  4. Lower back and hipsFocused attention on the lumbar area, which is where most desk and driving tension settles.
  5. Cool downLighter closing strokes, then time to sit up slowly and drink some water.

This is a good fit if

  • You carry tension in your shoulders, neck or lower back
  • You sit, drive or lift for most of your day
  • You want real pressure rather than a light spa stroke
  • You want one area worked properly instead of everything briefly

This is not for you if

  • You have an acute injury, recent surgery or unexplained back pain
  • You have a skin infection or open wound on the back
  • You are in the first trimester of pregnancy
  • You need medical diagnosis or treatment for a back condition

If anything on this list applies to you, call us before booking and we will talk it through honestly.

Questions

Back Massage FAQ

How much pressure will you use?

Whatever you ask for. We check in early and adjust. Deep does not have to mean painful — tell us if it crosses that line.

Will I be sore afterward?

After deeper work you may feel worked-on for a day, similar to after exercise. Water and a warm shower help.

Do I need to undress fully?

No. Only the back is exposed and you are draped throughout. You stay covered everywhere else.

Can I book this with a foot massage?

Yes — back massage and foot detox on the same visit is a common pairing and makes for a genuinely restful afternoon.
