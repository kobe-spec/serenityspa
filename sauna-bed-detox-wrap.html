About this treatment

What sauna bed detox is

Bentonite clay is applied over the body, then you rest inside an infrared sauna bed while the heat does the work. The combination is designed to encourage sweating, support circulation, and leave skin soft and clean. It is our most genuinely relaxing ninety minutes — warm, quiet and entirely passive. Castle Hayne clients often book it in the same week as a contouring session, or on its own when they simply want to sweat, decompress and reset after a heavy stretch.

Benefits

  • Encourages sweating and circulation through infrared heat
  • Supports the body’s natural cleansing processes
  • May reduce the feeling of water retention and bloating
  • Clay draws at surface impurities and leaves skin soft
  • Eases muscle tension and general stress

Your appointment

What to expect

  1. Hydrate firstWe start you with water — you will sweat, and going in dry is the main reason people feel wrung out afterward.
  2. Clay applicationBentonite clay is applied over the areas being wrapped.
  3. WrapThe treated areas are wrapped to hold heat and keep the clay in contact with the skin.
  4. Infrared sauna bedYou rest inside the bed at a temperature set to your comfort. Say the word at any point and we adjust or stop.
  5. Rinse and rehydrateClay is rinsed away, skin is dried, and we finish with more water and a rest before you drive.

Questions

Sauna Bed Detox FAQ

How hot does the sauna bed get?

It is set to your comfort, not a fixed number. Infrared heat feels gentler than a traditional sauna because it warms you rather than the air around you.

Will I lose weight from this?

Any immediate change on the scale is water you have sweated out and it returns when you rehydrate. This is a comfort and circulation treatment, not a weight-loss one.

What should I bring?

A water bottle and something loose to change into afterward. Skip heavy moisturiser or makeup on the areas being wrapped.

Can I do this alongside cavitation?

Yes, and it is a good pairing — though we usually schedule them on separate days so you are not overheated before contouring work.
