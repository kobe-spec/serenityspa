About this treatment

What face sculpting is

Face sculpting uses radio frequency, ultrasound and skin-tightening technology worked across the jawline, cheeks and neck. The warmth is intended to stimulate collagen and elastin, encourage circulation, and support firmer, more defined contours over a series of sessions. There are no needles, no injectables and nothing to recover from — clients from Castle Hayne and Porters Neck often book it on a lunch break. It takes an hour, and it is our most popular first service for anyone nervous about body treatments.

Benefits

  • Designed to lift and tighten areas of loose skin
  • Supports a more defined jawline and cheekbone contour
  • May soften the appearance of a double chin
  • Stimulates collagen and elastin activity
  • Softens the look of fine lines and improves skin firmness
  • No needles, no injectables, no downtime

Your appointment

What to expect

  1. Skin consultationWe look at your skin, discuss what you want to change, and check nothing rules out radio frequency.
  2. CleanseMakeup and product are removed and the skin is prepped with conductive gel.
  3. Radio frequency passThe handpiece moves in slow sections across the jaw, cheeks and neck. It feels warm, similar to a hot stone massage.
  4. Ultrasound and lifting workUltrasound is applied along the contour lines you want defined.
  5. FinishGel is removed, skin is soothed, and we go over sun protection and next steps.

Questions

Face Sculpting FAQ

Is this the same as a filler or Botox?

No. Nothing is injected. Face sculpting works with energy applied to the surface of the skin, so the effect is subtler and builds over sessions rather than appearing at once.

What does it feel like?

Warm and smooth. Most clients find it relaxing. If the heat ever feels sharp rather than warm, we turn it down immediately.

Can I wear makeup afterward?

Yes, though we suggest waiting a couple of hours. Skin may look slightly flushed for a short while, which settles.

How many sessions before I see something?

Most clients book a short series. A single visit can leave the face looking tighter for a day or two; lasting change needs repetition. Results vary.

Can I book it with another service?

Yes — it sits neatly alongside teeth whitening if you want a full refresh before an event.
