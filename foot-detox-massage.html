About this treatment

What foot detox is

This is the quietest service we offer. It starts with a warm foot soak, moves into gentle exfoliation to lift dry and rough skin, and finishes with a focused massage through the sole, arch, heel and ankle. It is built for people who stand all day — nurses, teachers, hospitality and trade workers across Castle Hayne and Wilmington. Seventy-five minutes, no technology, no contouring goals. You leave with feet that feel looked after rather than merely tolerated.

Benefits

  • Relieves tension through the arch, heel and ankle
  • Softens dry, rough and calloused skin
  • Encourages circulation in the lower legs and feet
  • A genuinely restful break from a day spent standing
  • No equipment, no aftercare, nothing to plan around

Questions

Foot Detox FAQ

What does a foot detox actually do?

It is a soak designed to feel cleansing and relaxing. We do not claim it removes toxins from your body — the value here is comfort, circulation and softer skin.

How hard is the massage pressure?

Entirely up to you. Say the word at any point and we adjust. Most clients want medium pressure through the arch and lighter work around the ankle.

Can I book this if I am diabetic?

Please check with your doctor first. If you have any loss of sensation or foot complications, we will not treat without clearance.

Do I need to do anything afterward?

No. Wear comfortable shoes home and drink some water. That is it.
